# Rename, edit and delete

Long-press any project, area or 360 card to open its menu. In Tile view you can also tap the three dots (⋮) on the card.

## Rename or edit

1. Choose **Rename**.
2. Change the **Title** and, if you like, the **Description**.
3. Tap **Save**. The title can't be left empty.

## Delete

Choose **Delete**. A message says **Deleted** with an **UNDO** button. Tap **UNDO** within a few seconds to bring it back; after that it is gone for good.

- Deleting a **project** also deletes all its areas and the 360s in them.
- Deleting an **area** also deletes the 360s in it.

## Other menu options

- **Project:** **Favorite** / **Unfavorite**, **Add Location** / **Change Location**, **Share**.
- **Area:** **Share**.
- **360:** **Move** (to another area in the same project), **Set area cover** and **Set project cover** (stitched 360s only), **Share**, **Add to stitching queue** or **Stitch again**, **View raw data** (while the original shots are still on the phone), and **Info** (name, date, description).

If a stitch option is greyed out, the reason is written under it.
