# Stitching on your phone

Stitching joins the shots from a capture into one 360° image. Bluez360 does it right on your phone, and it usually takes a few minutes.

Choose how it works in **Account Settings › Stitching**:

- **On phone** (the default) — your phone stitches the 360. After a Photo capture it goes straight into the stitching queue. After a video sweep you pick **Stitch Now** or **Queue it, stitch later**.
- **Capture only** — your shots are saved but not stitched. The 360's card shows a few of its shots with **Ready to stitch**, and the **Stitching Queue** menu item is hidden.

To stitch a saved capture later, switch to **On phone**, long-press the 360 and tap **Add to stitching queue**.

Stitching keeps going if you leave the app. A notification shows each step, and another tells you when the 360 is ready.

Each finished stitch uses 1 token. A failed or cancelled stitch uses none. If you run out, a 360 stitched straight after capture shows as a locked preview until you unlock it, and **Add to stitching queue** is greyed out until you get a token.

If **On phone** is greyed out, this phone can't stitch 360s itself.

## Stitching a 360 again

Why stitch again? Every app update can bring stitching fixes and improvements. Stitching an older 360 again runs the newer stitching on its original shots, so it can come out better than before.

To stitch a finished 360 again, long-press it and tap **Stitch again**. It needs the 360's original shots still on the phone, and it costs another token.
