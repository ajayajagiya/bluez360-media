# Set a project's location

A project can have a place on the map. Only projects have one; areas and 360s don't.

**Why add one?** The project's map shows in Reel view, and you can sort your projects by **Location** in the Gallery.

## Open the map

- Long-press the project in the Gallery and choose **Add Location** (or **Change Location** if it already has one).
- In Reel view, you can also tap **Add Location** on the card, or tap its map.
- In Reel view, **Add New Project** opens the map straight away.

## Pick the place

Use one of these:

- **Search:** type in **Search location** and tap a suggestion. Search needs an internet connection.
- **Drop a pin:** long-press anywhere on the map.
- **Where you are:** tap the round location button (⌖, **Use my current location**) on the map. The app asks for location permission when you tap it. If Location is switched off, it offers to open your phone's settings.

## Confirm

A **Use this location?** card appears with a **Title** and **Description**. Edit them if you want, then tap **Use this**.

- A place from search renames the project and replaces its description.
- For a dropped pin or your current location, the title starts blank. Leave it blank to keep the project's name and save only the spot, or type a name to rename the project.

Tap **Skip for now** to leave without setting a place.
