# Delete your account

Deleting your account is permanent. It can't be undone.

**Before you delete:** unused tokens are lost, and your .bz360 backups stop opening. To keep your 360°s as pictures that open anywhere, send them out first with **Share image**.

## In the app

1. Tap your account icon at the top of the screen, then **Account Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete Account**.
3. Tick **I understand this is permanent**.
4. Tap **Delete My Account**.

If you signed in a while ago, Google may ask you to choose your account again first. When it's done you'll see "Account deleted" and go back to the sign-in screen.

## On the web

Tap **Or request deletion on our web page**, or visit bluez360.web.app/delete-account on any device. If you can't sign in at all, email **bluez360.studio@gmail.com** from the address on the account and we'll delete it for you.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in.
- Your token balance. Unused tokens are lost.
- Your invite code, and the record of ads you watched for tokens.

## What happens to your things

- **Your projects, areas and photos stay on this phone.** They were never uploaded, so they aren't deleted. Sign in again with the same Google account to see them.
- **Your .bz360 backups stop opening**, for good – even if you sign up again with the same email.
- Signing up again with the same email doesn't give you the welcome tokens a second time.
