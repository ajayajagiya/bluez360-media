# Explore, Gallery and Queue

The bar at the bottom of the screen has three tabs:

- **Explore** — 360s to look at. For now, the three demo 360s that come with the app.
- **Gallery** — your own projects, areas and 360s. The app opens here.
- **Queue** — the stitching queue: what's stitching, waiting, finished or failed.

## Moving between them

- Tap a tab to switch. Each tab remembers where you were, so a project you left open in **Gallery** is still open when you come back from **Queue**.
- Tap the tab you're already on to go back to its first screen. In **Gallery**, that takes you out of a project straight to your project list.
- The phone's **Back** button on **Explore** or **Queue** takes you to **Gallery**.

The bar stays at the bottom inside your projects and areas. It hides while you capture a 360 and in the 360 viewer, and comes back when you leave them.

## Your picture, top right

Tap your picture at the top right of the search bar to open **Account Settings**. That's where you'll find your tokens, the app's options, **Log out**, **Help & support**, **Privacy Policy** and **Delete Account**.
