# Settings

To open Settings, tap your picture at the top right of the screen. It opens **Account Settings** straight away.

At the top you'll see your Google name, email and picture, with a small **Log out** button under them, and three counts: **Projects**, **360s stitched** and **Tokens**. Tap **Tokens** to open the Tokens screen.

## Options

Each choice is saved straight away.

- **Theme** – **Dark** (the default) or **Light**.
- **Capture mode** – **Video** or **Photo**. If your phone can't record video well enough, **Video** is greyed out with the reason underneath.
- **Lens** – **Ultrawide** or **Main**. Shown only in Video mode. **Ultrawide** is greyed out if your phone doesn't have one the app can use.
- **Shutter** – **Auto** or **Manual**. Shown only in Photo mode.
- **Stitching** – **Capture only** or **On phone** (the default).
- **Stitching quality** – **HD** or **4K**. **4K** is greyed out when your phone can't do it, with the reason underneath.

## Buttons at the bottom

Three buttons side by side:

- **Help & support** – opens **Blueezee**, a chat that answers your questions from these help pages.
- **Privacy Policy** – opens our privacy policy in your browser.
- **Delete Account** – permanently deletes your account.

Under them, last on the screen, **Find us** has links to Bluez360's **Website**, Google **Business** page, Google **Maps**, **Instagram** and **YouTube**.

**Invite friends** is on the Tokens screen: tap the **Tokens** card, then **Invite a friend**. The demo 360s are in the **Explore** tab.
