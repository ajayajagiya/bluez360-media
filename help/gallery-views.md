# Reel, Tile and Grid views

Each level has its own set of views. Tap one on the switch under the add button, or swipe left or right across the list to move to the next one.

- **Gallery (projects):** **Reel**, **Tile** and **Grid**. Reel shows one big card at a time with the project's map; swipe up and down to move between projects.
- **Project (areas):** **Tile** and **Grid**.
- **Area (360s):** **Reel** and **Grid**.

In Tile and Grid, a pin icon shows whether a project has a location yet (filled means it has one). Project cards in Reel and Tile show the date and how many areas they hold; area cards in Tile show how many 360s.

## Search

Type in the search bar at the top. It finds names and descriptions on the level you are on: projects in the Gallery, areas inside a project (**Search in** the project name), and 360s inside an area.

## Sort

Tap the sort button to the right of the view switch:

- **Date** — newest first (the default).
- **Name** — A to Z.
- **Location** — Gallery only. Projects with a place come first, in order of place name; projects without one go to the bottom.
