# Permissions the app asks for

After you first sign in, a **Before you start** screen explains three permissions. Tap **Continue** to answer Android's questions, or **Not now** to skip for now.

- **Camera** – to capture your 360°s. Without it you can't capture, but you can still view, share and back up.
- **Location** – to pin each 360° on the map. It's only used while you're using the app, never in the background. It's optional: you can capture without it.
- **Notifications** – to tell you when a stitch is done.

## If you said no

- **Camera or Notifications off:** the **Before you start** screen comes back the next time you open the app. Location never brings it back.
- **Starting a capture without the camera:** the app asks for camera access again. If it's turned off for good, a message offers **SETTINGS** so you can switch it on.
- **Capturing without location:** you may see **Allow location?**. You can close it and keep capturing; that 360° is just saved without a place.
- **Android stopped asking:** after you refuse twice, Android won't show the question again. The button then says **Open settings**. Turn the permission on for Bluez360 there, then come back to the app.

You can change any of these later in your phone's settings.
