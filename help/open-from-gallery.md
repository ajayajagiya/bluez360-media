# Open a 360 from your phone's gallery

You can look around in a 360 photo that is already on your phone, even one made with another app or camera.

1. Open the photo in your phone's gallery or file manager.
2. Choose "Open with" and pick Bluez360, or tap Share and pick Bluez360. (The exact words depend on your phone.)
3. The photo opens in the Bluez360 360 viewer, with all the usual controls.

If you are not signed in yet, it opens once you have signed in.

## Good to know

- It only views the photo. Nothing is added to your projects, and no token is used.
- It works with JPEG photos only.
- Bluez360 checks first that the photo really is a 360. A normal flat photo is refused with "That isn't a 360° photo — Bluez360 opens equirectangular panoramas." A 360 is a photo that is exactly twice as wide as it is tall, or one marked as a 360 (photo sphere) by the app that made it.
- Your phone lists Bluez360 for every JPEG, not only 360s, because Android can't tell the difference. That's why the check above is needed.
