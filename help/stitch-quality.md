# HD or 4K

Choose in **Account Settings › Stitching quality**.

- **HD** (the default) makes a 360 image 5120 × 2560 pixels.
- **4K** makes it 8192 × 4096 pixels. It's sharper when you zoom in, but needs much more memory and space while stitching.

Both cost 1 token per stitch.

**Which to choose?** HD suits most 360s and is lighter on your phone. Pick 4K when people will zoom in on small details, like signs or labels.

**Which phones can do 4K?** It needs at least 6 GB of RAM plus enough free space. The more RAM your phone has, the less free space it needs; a phone with 6 GB of RAM needs more than 16 GB free. If your phone doesn't qualify, **4K** is greyed out and a note shows your phone's numbers. The check runs again each time you open Settings, so freeing up space can unlock it. If the note says RAM is the problem, freeing space won't help.

**Low on space later?** If you chose 4K and your phone has since filled up, adding a 360 to the queue shows **Low on space for 4K**. Pick **Stitch in HD** or **Continue in 4K** (the stitch may fail).

The quality is fixed when a 360 joins the queue. Changing the setting doesn't change stitches already queued, and **Restitch** keeps the original quality.

To redo a failed 4K stitch in HD: switch to **HD**, tap **Remove** on the failed job, then long-press the 360 and tap **Add to stitching queue**.
