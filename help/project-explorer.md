# The Project explorer

The Project explorer is a side panel for jumping straight to another project or area, without going back through each screen.

## Open it

On the Gallery, a project or an area, either:

- swipe in from the left edge of the screen, or
- tap the menu button (☰) at the left end of the search bar.

## Use it

1. Every project is listed. Tap a project's row to show its areas; tap it again to hide them. Only one project opens at a time.
2. Tap an area to go straight into it. The number beside each area is how many 360s it holds.
3. To open the project itself, tap the eye button on the right of its row.

The panel opens on the project you are in (or were in last) and marks the area you were in last. 360s are not listed here, only projects and areas.

## The Home button

When you open the explorer from a project or an area, a house button appears at the top right of the panel. Tap it to go back to the Gallery in one step.

After a jump, your phone's back button still takes you up one level: from an area to its project, then to the Gallery.
