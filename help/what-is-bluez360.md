# What Bluez360 does

Bluez360 turns your Android phone into a 360° camera. You stand in one spot and turn with your phone, and the app joins the shots into one 360° image you can look all around in.

What you can do:

1. **Capture** a 360°. In **Photo** mode you follow dots on the screen. In **Video** mode you record one slow sweep.
2. **Stitch** it on your phone. Stitching joins your shots into one 360°. Each stitch uses one token.
3. **View** it. Look around, zoom in, or let it turn by itself.
4. **Share** the image with other apps, or **back up** a 360°, an area or a whole project to a file on your phone.

Your 360s are kept in **projects** (for example, a shop or a house), with **areas** inside them (like Kitchen or Entrance).

Your photos stay on your phone. They are never uploaded to us.

The app needs Android 12 or newer, and a Google account to sign in. Want a look first? Open **Demos** from your profile picture to see sample 360s.
