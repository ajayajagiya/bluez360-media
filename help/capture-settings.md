# Capture settings

Tap your profile picture (top right), then **Account Settings**. Every choice is remembered.

**Capture mode — Video or Photo.** **Video** (the default) records the 360 in a few continuous turns. **Photo** takes 66 single shots as you follow dots, at your camera's full resolution. If your phone can't record 1080p video, **Video** is greyed out with the reason underneath.

**Lens — Ultrawide or Main** (Video mode only). **Main** is the default. **Ultrawide** sees more, so it usually needs fewer sweeps. It's greyed out if your phone has no ultrawide camera the app can use. If the ultrawide won't start during a capture, the app offers **Use main camera**.

**Shutter — Auto or Manual** (Photo mode only). With **Auto**, holding the dot inside the centre ring takes the shot for you. With **Manual**, nothing happens until you tap the shutter button.

On the Photo capture screen, the buttons at the top right turn a horizon line and a grid on or off. These are remembered too.

There is no flash or HDR setting.

Where stitching runs, and HD or 4K, are set on the same screen.
