# Capture settings

Tap your account icon (top right), then **Account Settings**. Every choice is remembered.

**Capture mode — Video or Photo.** **Video** (the default) records the 360 in a few continuous turns. **Photo** takes 66 single shots as you follow dots, at your camera's full resolution. If your phone can't record 1080p video, **Video** is greyed out with the reason underneath.

**Which mode to choose?** Video is quicker: a few slow turns instead of 66 shots. Photo takes longer, but every shot uses your camera's full resolution.

**Lens — Ultrawide or Main** (Video mode only). **Main** is the default. **Ultrawide** sees more, so it usually needs fewer sweeps; pick it for a quicker capture. It's greyed out if your phone has no ultrawide camera the app can use. If the ultrawide won't start during a capture, the app offers **Use main camera**.

**Ultrawide is there but the camera won't open?** Some phones have an ultrawide camera but don't let other apps record video with it. The phone's own camera app can use it, but Bluez360 can't, and there is no setting in Bluez360 or on the phone that changes this. Tap **Use main camera** (or choose **Main** under **Lens**) and capture as normal; it just needs a few more sweeps.

**Shutter — Auto or Manual** (Photo mode only). With **Auto**, holding the dot inside the centre ring takes the shot for you. With **Manual**, nothing happens until you tap the shutter button; use it if you'd rather choose the moment each shot is taken.

On the Photo capture screen, the buttons at the top right turn a horizon line and a grid on or off. These are remembered too.

There is no flash or HDR setting.

Where stitching runs, and HD or 4K, are set on the same screen.
