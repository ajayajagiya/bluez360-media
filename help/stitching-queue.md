# The stitching queue

Tap your profile picture (top right), then **Stitching Queue**. You can also tap **View** after a capture. It only appears when stitching is set to **On phone**.

There are two tabs, **Active** and **Completed**; tap or swipe between them. One 360 stitches at a time.

Cards in **Active** show:

- **Stitching** — the picture sharpens with a percentage and the current step, like "3/10 · Finding where the photos overlap". **Choosing where to join the photos** is the slowest step and can sit still for minutes.
- **Waiting its turn** — queued behind another stitch.
- **Paused — waiting for you** — the queue is paused.
- **Failed** — the reason in red, with **Retry** and **Remove**.

**Cancel** stops a stitch or takes it out of the queue. **Pause the queue** lets the running stitch finish, then starts nothing new until you tap **Start stitching**.

In **Completed**, tap **Preview**, **Restitch** (costs 1 token) or **Remove**.

**If a stitch fails:**

- **The shots could not be lined up** — capture again, keeping the phone level and pausing on each dot.
- **The phone ran out of memory** — close other apps and tap **Retry**. If it was 4K, try HD.
- **Stitching was interrupted** — tap **Retry**.
- **Some of the captured photos are missing** — capture this 360 again.
- Anything else — tap **Retry**, or capture again.

A failed stitch uses no token.
