# Signing in

Bluez360 signs you in with your Google account. There is no separate password, and no guest mode.

1. Open the app.
2. Tap **Continue with Google**.
3. Pick the Google account you want to use.

After that you stay signed in, even when you close the app.

## Why sign-in is needed

- **It keeps your work separate.** Each Google account has its own projects and 360s on the phone, so two people sharing a phone don't see each other's work.
- **Your tokens and invite code belong to your account.** So do your **.bz360** backups: only your account can open them.
- **Your photos are not uploaded.** Signing in doesn't send your pictures anywhere. They stay on your phone.

## Logging out

Tap your account icon at the top of the screen, tap **Logout**, then confirm with **Log out**. Your projects stay on the phone. Sign in again with the same account to see them.

## Switching accounts

To use a different Google account, log out (see above), tap **Continue with Google** and pick the other account. Each account keeps its own projects, 360s and tokens, so switching back shows your work again.

## If sign-in doesn't work

- "Sign-in failed. Check your connection." means the app couldn't reach Google. Check you're online and try again.
- If you close the Google account list, nothing happens. Just tap **Continue with Google** again.
