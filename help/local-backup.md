# Back up to your phone

**Local backup** saves a 360°, an area or a whole project as one **.bz360** file in the **Download/Bluez360** folder on your phone. It is a backup for you: it opens only in Bluez360, signed in to the same account that made it.

## Make a backup

1. Press and hold the 360°, area or project to open its menu.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Local backup**.
3. Choose what to include:
   - **Raw data**: the original shots (or video) the 360° was stitched from. Keep these if you may want to stitch again.
   - **Final 360°**: the finished 360° picture.
4. Tap **Back up**.

The backup runs in the background. Progress shows in your notifications, with a **Cancel** button. When it's done you'll get "… saved" with a **Share** button, so you can also send the file somewhere safe, like your cloud storage.

Names, dates and other details always go in. Locked 360°s are included and stay locked.

## If you can't back up

- A choice is greyed out when there's nothing of that kind, with the reason: **Not stitched yet**, **Deleted from this phone** or **Nothing captured yet**.
- "Needs … This phone has … free." Free up some space first.
- "Connect to the internet once to set up backups." Go online once, then try again.

If you delete your account, your backups will no longer open.
