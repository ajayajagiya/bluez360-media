# Earning tokens

You can get tokens for free in two ways. Open the Tokens screen (tap your account icon, then **Account Settings**, then the **Tokens** card) and look under **Earn free**.

## Watch an ad

1. Tap **Watch an ad**.
2. Watch the ad to the end.
3. You will see "Ad finished. Your token is on its way." Your balance goes up by 1 a moment later.

- You can earn up to 30 tokens a day from ads. After 30, **Watch an ad** turns grey and says "Come back tomorrow". It's back at 5:30 AM India time.
- If you close the ad early, there is no token.
- If no ad plays, the app tells you why: you're offline, an ad blocker is on, or no ad is available right now. Try again in a few minutes.

You can also tap **Watch an ad** on the **Unlock this 360°** screen. The token you earn is then used to unlock that 360°.

## Invite a friend

Tap **Invite a friend** to share your invite code. When your friend uses it and finishes their first real 360° stitch, you both get 5 tokens.

New accounts also start with 10 free tokens. Not enough? You can also [buy a pack](buy-tokens.md).
