# Tour Builder

**Tour Builder** turns a project into a virtual tour on Google Maps. Each 360° is placed where it was taken, faces the right way and is linked to the next one, so people on Google Maps can tap an arrow and walk from room to room. It works on a whole project, not on an area or a single 360°.

## Open it

- Press and hold a project, then tap **Tour Builder** (after **Share**).
- Or open the Project explorer (☰) and tap the Tour Builder button on the project's row.

If the project has no location yet, you'll see **Where is this property?** Tap **Add location**, or **Add from Google Maps** to start from 360°s you've already published.

Only stitched 360°s are in Tour Builder. They're numbered 1, 2, 3… across the project, area by area, and the same number is on the map. A locked 360° shows a lock: tap it to unlock it the usual way.

Tour Builder opens on the first step that isn't done yet. Everything is saved as you go. **Back to project**, at the bottom of the menu, leaves it.

## The menu

Tap ☰ for the steps. Each one gets a tick when it's done.

- **Prep tour:** **Blur**, for faces and number plates.
- **Build tour:** **Place**, **Link** and **Align**.
- **Publish tour:** **Review & publish**, **Google Maps status** and **Add from Google Maps**.

Your tour tokens are beside your name. Tap them to get more.

## The map

Dots are 360°s, and lines are links. **Place**, **Link** and **Align** are three tabs on the same map. At the right of the tabs:

- The **map** button: Roadmap, Satellite, Hybrid or Terrain. Your choice is kept.
- The **floor** button shows one floor at a time. 0 is the ground floor, 1 and up are above it, and −1 to −3 are below. Use **Add floor above** or **Add floor below**. A one-storey place never needs it.

Lost your tour after zooming or scrolling? The round button at the bottom right of the map, **Back to your tour**, brings every dot on this floor back into view.

Tap a dot to see what you can do with it. Messages show in one line at the bottom. After you remove something, it offers **Undo** for 5 seconds.

## Place

The strip under the map holds your 360°s, and placed ones have a tick. Each dot starts where your phone's GPS put it, so you usually only nudge it. A 360° with no GPS fix waits in the strip until you place it.

- Drag a thumbnail onto the map, or tap it to drop it in the middle of the map.
- Drag a dot to where you stood.
- To put a 360° on another floor, tap its dot, or press and hold its thumbnail, and tap **Move to floor**. Then place it on that floor.
- **Remove from map** takes the dot off the map. It never removes anything from Google Maps.

360°s you don't place are left out of what's published.

## Link

Tap a dot, tap **Link**, then tap another dot. A link works both ways. **Auto-link nearest**, under the map, links each dot on the floor you're looking at to its closest neighbours.

- To link two floors, tap **Link to another floor**, then the dot on the other floor. Both dots get a floor badge.
- To remove one link, tap its line, then the red **✕** in its middle. **Remove all links** in a dot's menu removes that dot's links.
- **Remove all links** under the map removes every link in the tour, on every floor. **Undo** brings them back for a few seconds.

## Align

Align makes each 360° face the right way. The map is at the top, with a cone from the dot. The 360° is below it, with a mark in the middle.

1. Swipe the 360°, or drag the round handle at the tip of the cone, until what's under the mark is what the cone points at on the map.
2. Tap **Confirm**. The dot gets a tick.
3. Tap the next button, at the bottom right, for the next 360°.

Each 360° starts facing where your phone's compass said, so you usually only check it. The small compass on the map points north: tap it to turn the map north-up. **Reset direction** goes back to what the compass said.
