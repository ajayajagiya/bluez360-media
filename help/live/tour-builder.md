# Tour Builder

**Tour Builder** turns a project into a virtual tour on Google Maps. Each 360° is placed where it was taken, faces the right way and is linked to the next one, so people on Google Maps can tap an arrow and walk from room to room. It works on a whole project, not on an area or a single 360°.

## Open it

- Press and hold a project, then tap **Tour Builder** (after **Share**).
- Or open the Project explorer (☰) and tap the Tour Builder button on the project's row.

If the project has no location yet, you'll see **Where is this property?** Tap **Add location**, or **Add from Google Maps** to start from 360°s you've already published.

Only stitched 360°s are in Tour Builder. They're numbered 1, 2, 3… across the project, by name from A to Z, and the same number is on the map. To number them another way, tap **⋮** at the top right of Place, Link, Align or Review & publish: **Name A–Z**, **Name Z–A**, **Oldest first** or **Newest first**. Your choice is kept for every project.

A locked 360° shows a lock. Tap **Unlock** under the map in Place, or tap it on a Prep tour screen.

Tour Builder opens on the first step that isn't done yet. Everything is saved as you go. **Back to project**, at the bottom of the menu, leaves it.

## The menu

Tap ☰ for the steps. Each one gets a tick when it's done.

- **Prep tour:** **Nadir fix** for the tripod and its shadow, **Colour** for the tour's look ([how](tour-prep.md)), then **Blur** for faces and number plates.
- **Build tour:** **Place**, **Link** and **Align**.
- **Publish tour:** **Review & publish**, **Google Maps status** and **Add from Google Maps**.

Your tour tokens are beside your name. Tap them to get more.

## The map

Dots are 360°s, and lines are links. **Place**, **Link** and **Align** are three tabs on the same map. At the right of the tabs:

- The **map** button: Roadmap, Satellite, Hybrid or Terrain. Your choice is kept.
- The **floor** button shows one floor at a time. 0 is the ground floor, 1 and up are above it, and −1 to −3 are below. Use **Add floor above** or **Add floor below**. A one-storey place never needs it.
- **Remove floor 2**, at the bottom of that list, removes the floor you're looking at. It asks first. Its 360°s wait under Not placed on the next floor towards the ground (floor 0 for a basement), ready to place again, and their links to other floors go. **Undo** brings it all back. Floor 0 can't be removed.

The compass at the top left of the map points north. Tap it to turn the map north-up. Lost your tour after zooming or scrolling? The round button at the bottom right of the map, **Back to your tour**, brings every dot on this floor back into view.

Tap a dot to pick it. It stays picked on all three tabs, and the buttons under the map work on it.

The line above the map tells you what's going on, with a green tick when the 360° you picked is done for this tab. **Undo**, at its left, takes back your last change on this tab, and **Redo**, at its right, puts it back. Each tab has its own. They last while you're on the map: going to another step clears them. Unlocking, the order, the floor you're looking at and a new floor aren't undone.

## Place

The map is at the top. Under it is one 360° at a time, the one you picked: swipe it to look round, and use **‹ ›** at its sides to go through this floor's 360°s. Each dot starts where your phone's GPS put it, so you usually only nudge it.

- **Add to map** puts the 360° where its GPS says, or in the middle of the map if it has none. Then drag its dot into place.
- Drag a dot to where you stood.
- **Remove from map** takes the dot off the map. It never removes anything from Google Maps.
- To put a 360° on another floor, tap **To floor 1** (there's a button for each floor). It goes there unplaced, and its links stay. Switch to that floor with the floor button and tap **Add to map**.

360°s you don't place are left out of what's published. If you go on to Link or Align with some unplaced, the line above the map says how many.

## Link

A link works both ways.

- Tap a dot, tap **Start linking**, then tap the next dots in turn. Each one links to the one before, like a path. Tap **Stop** when you're done. Changing the floor stops it too.
- To link two floors, tap a dot, tap **Link to another floor**, pick the floor, then tap the dot there. Both dots get a floor badge. **Unlink floor 1** removes that link.
- To remove one link, tap its line, then the red **✕** in its middle. **Remove this dot's links** removes every link of the dot you picked.
- **Auto-link nearest** links each dot on the floor you're looking at to its closest neighbours.
- **Remove all links** removes every link on the floor you're looking at, and its links to other floors. It asks first, and **Undo** brings them back.

## Align

Align makes each 360° face the right way, one floor at a time. The map is at the top, with a cone from the dot. The 360° is below it, with a mark in the middle. Between them are the 360°'s number and name, with **‹ ›** at its sides, then **Confirm** and **Reset direction**.

1. Swipe the 360° until a door or window is under the mark. The cone turns with it.
2. If the cone doesn't point at that door or window on the map, drag the round handle at the tip of the cone until it does.
3. Tap **Confirm**. The dot gets a tick.
4. Tap **›** for the next 360° on this floor.

Each 360° starts facing where your phone's compass said, so you usually only check it. Line it up against the compass on the map. **Reset direction** goes back to what the compass said.
