# Your privacy

## What stays on your phone

- Your photos and finished 360s. They are never uploaded to us. Only the ones you publish to Google Maps leave your phone, and they go to your own Google account (see below).
- 360s you import from your phone, with the place and date read from them. Only the photos you pick are read.
- Your projects and areas, with their names, notes and places, except the place shown in Explore for a 360 you publish (see below).
- Your **.bz360** backups. We don't get a copy.

## What we keep

- Your Google email and account ID.
- Your tokens, your invite code, the code you were invited with, and when you joined.
- Google Play receipts for token packs, so one can't be used twice. We never see your card.
- A record of ads you watched for tokens, so one isn't paid twice.
- The code that lets your .bz360 backups open.
- Crash reports. They don't include your photos, location or email.
- One report of what your phone model's cameras can do, not linked to you.
- A short Explore entry for each 360 you publish to Google Maps (see below).

We sell nothing, build no profile of you, and don't track you across other apps.

## Google Maps

Nothing goes to Google Maps until you tap **Publish** in **Tour Builder**. Then your 360s, with your blurs, positions, directions, floors and links, and your project's place when you picked it from the search, go from your phone straight to Google, under your own Google account. We never get a copy. Once Google puts one Live, anyone on Google Maps can see it, with your Google name.

For each 360 you publish, we keep a short entry for Bluez360's **Explore**: its place and position, the dates it was taken and published, your Google name, a link to your Google picture, and a link to Google's small picture of the 360. Once Google puts it Live, everyone using Bluez360 can see it in Explore. The place is your project's place, or the project's name if that place is a pin with no name. There's no switch to turn this off. To have one taken out, email us. **Remove from Google Maps** removes its entry too, and one Google turns down is taken out the next time the app checks with Google.

When you look at Explore, its pictures and 360s come straight from Google to your phone, as on Google Maps, and the app asks Google which ones are Live. We keep no record of what you look at.

Faces are found on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to find them. 360s you add from Google Maps come straight from your Google account to your phone, and get no Explore entry.

## Blueezee

Questions you type to **Blueezee** are sent to Google Gemini to write the answer. Google may use them to improve its products, and people at Google may read them, so don't type personal details.

Your chat is kept on your phone only, for 7 days after your last message, so you can come back to an answer. To remove it sooner, tap **⋮** then **Clear chat**. Signing out also clears it. We don't save chats on our servers; we only keep a count of your questions today.

## When you delete your account

Everything linked to your account is deleted, except purchase receipts (unlinked from you, for Google Play refunds) and a one-way code made from your email, so welcome tokens can't be claimed twice. Crash reports are removed after 90 days. Your Explore entries are deleted too, but 360s you published stay on Google Maps: take them down first with **Remove from Google Maps** in Tour Builder.

Full policy: **Privacy Policy** in Settings.
