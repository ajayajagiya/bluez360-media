# Blur faces and number plates

Google Maps turns down 360°s that show people's faces or car number plates. **Blur** is the first step of a new tour.

Blurs go only on Tour Builder's copy of each 360°. Your 360° in the project stays as it was, and so do the gallery, **Share image** and your backups. Faces are found on your phone: nothing is sent anywhere to find them.

## The Blur screen

Each 360° in the tour shows its state: **Not checked**, **N blurred** or **Nothing to blur**. **Check again** means it was stitched again since, so open it and check the blurs still fit. Tap a 360° to open it.

## Blur a 360°

The whole 360° is at the top. Below it is a close-up of the spot you pick.

1. Tap **Detect** to find the faces. Each one shows as a numbered dot.
2. Tap a dot to see it close up. Drag the shape to move it, drag a corner to resize it, or drag the round handle under it to turn it. Pinch the close-up to zoom in, up to 3 times, to put a blur exactly where you want it. The **✕** closes the close-up and sets the zoom back.
3. Tap **Apply** to blur it, or **Apply all** at the top right to blur every face found.

Detect finds faces only. For a number plate, or anything Detect missed, tap that spot in the 360° at the top, then **Oval** or **Box**. Fit the shape and tap **Apply**.

- **Remove** takes a shape off, blurred or not.
- **Preview** shows the 360° exactly as it will go to Google, with only the applied blurs.
- **Original** takes every blur off this 360°.

**Apply**, **Apply all**, **Remove** and **Original** each offer **Undo** for 5 seconds.

If you see "Face finding is still getting ready", wait a minute and try again.

## When you're done

Tap **Mark Blur done**. You don't have to check every 360°, and this is also how you skip Blur. Next time, Tour Builder opens on the map. A 360° added to the project later shows as **Not checked**.

## Already on Google Maps?

Google can't change the picture of a published 360°. A new blur means taking it off Google Maps and publishing it again, so its views start again from zero. You're asked first, at **Review & publish**, with **Publish again**.

360°s added from Google Maps can't be blurred. They show **From Google Maps**.
