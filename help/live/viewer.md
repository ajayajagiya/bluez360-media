# The 360 viewer

Open an area and tap a 360 to view it. A percentage shows while it loads.

- **Look around:** drag anywhere on the photo.
- **Zoom:** pinch, or use the buttons below.
- **Hide the controls:** tap the photo once. Tap again to bring them back.

## The buttons

From left to right along the bottom:

- **Zoom in** and **Zoom out**. Zooming out goes wider than the normal view.
- **Turn 90°** — turns the view a quarter turn to the right.
- **Auto-rotate** — the 360 spins slowly on its own. Tap again (**Stop rotating**) to stop. The screen stays on while it spins, so it's handy for showing a 360 to someone, or leaving it running on a display.
- **Compass mode** — look around by moving your phone, as if you were standing there. It's the most natural way to explore a place. Tap again to turn it off. Auto-rotate and compass mode can't run together; turning one on turns the other off.
- **Reset view** — back to the starting angle and normal zoom, and stops auto-rotate.

## 360s from Explore

A 360 that someone published to Google Maps opens from **Explore** in Google's Street View viewer, inside the app. You look around and zoom the same way, with the same buttons. The differences:

- At the top, a card shows who published it and the place. Tapping the photo hides it along with the buttons.
- It's just that one 360: there are no arrows to walk to others nearby.
- **Zoom out** stops at Google's widest view.
- It's never locked, and it needs a connection. Only the part you're looking at is loaded.
- Press your phone's **Back** to close it, back to the same place in the list.
- **This 360° is not on Google Maps any more**: the person who published it took it down, or Google did.

## If you see a message instead

- **Locked preview**: the 360 is blurred until you unlock it with one token. Tap **Unlock**.
- "Not been stitched yet": the shots are safe on the phone. Add the 360 to the stitching queue first.
- "Damaged and cannot be opened": stitch it again from the raw shots.
