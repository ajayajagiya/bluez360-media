# Share a 360° as a video

**Share as video** turns one 360° into a short, tall video that looks around the room once, slowly. You can post it where a 360° photo can't go, such as a reel, a status or a business page.

1. Press and hold a 360° to open its menu.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Tap **Share as video**.
4. Wait while it says **Making video**. The bar and the small preview show how far it has got.

When it's done, the menu closes and you'll see **Saved to Download/Bluez360/Video**, with the file's name.

## The video

- It's 18 seconds long and tall (9:16), 1080 × 1920, like a reel.
- It turns once all the way round, starting from the middle of the 360°, and ends where it began.
- It shows more of the floor and ceiling than a normal phone video.
- It has no sound.
- It's up to about 67 MB.

It fits Google Business Profile's limits for videos: up to 30 seconds and 75 MB.

## Good to know

- **Keep Bluez360 open** until it's done. To stop, tap **Cancel**. Nothing is saved.
- While it's being made, the menu can only be closed with **Cancel**.
- The video is named after the 360°. Making another of the same 360° keeps both: the new one gets a number, such as "Kitchen (1).mp4".
- Your 360° itself isn't changed.
- **Share as video** is for one 360° at a time. It isn't in the menu of an area or a project.

## When you can't use it

**Share as video** is greyed out, with the reason under it, when:

- **Locked**: the 360° isn't unlocked yet. Unlock it first.
- **Not stitched yet**: the 360° hasn't been made yet.
- **Deleted from this phone**: its picture isn't on this phone any more.

If something goes wrong, you'll see **Could not make the video**. If your phone is full, you'll see **Not enough space for the video**: free some space and try again.
