# Add 360s from Google Maps

Already have 360°s on Google Maps, made with any app? Bring them into a project and build your tour from them.

1. In Tour Builder, open the menu (☰) and tap **Add from Google Maps**. A project with no location offers it too.
2. Your published 360°s are listed, with their place, date and views. You can search by place.
3. Tap the download button on each one you want. You can download several at once. Stay on the screen until they've finished.
4. Tap **Add N to project**. Tour Builder goes to the **Place** tab.

Only 360°s published with your own Google account are listed: the one you're signed in to Bluez360 with.

## What you get

- Each one arrives as a new 360°, named after its place, or **From Google Maps**. It's already placed, facing the way Google has it, on Google's floor, and linked to the others you added with it.
- Its Google Maps status shows straight away.
- No tokens. They're never locked.
- They can't be stitched again or blurred, because there are no original shots.
- If the project has no area, a **New Area** is made for them. A project with no location gets one from them.
- They aren't listed in Bluez360's Explore.

Changes you make, like moving, linking or aligning, are sent to the same photos on Google Maps when you publish. The picture isn't uploaded again. Taking one off the map, or out of the project, never removes it from Google Maps.
