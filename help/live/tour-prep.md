# Remove the tripod, and colour your tour

Before a tour goes on Google Maps, **Prep tour** gets its 360°s ready, in three steps: **Nadir fix**, **Colour**, then **Blur** ([Blur faces and number plates](tour-blur.md)). A new tour opens on Nadir fix.

Each step works the same way. First it does all the 360°s in one go, then you open only the ones that need more and fix them by hand.

Everything goes on Tour Builder's own copy of each 360°. Your 360° in the project stays as it was, and so do the gallery, **Share image** and your backups of it. The copy keeps all of your 360°'s details, such as where and when it was taken, and its full quality.

## Nadir fix: the tripod and its shadow

The ground straight under the camera shows the tripod, and on a sunny day its long shadow. Nadir fix fills them in with what the ground around them looks like. It's done on your phone, so nothing is sent anywhere.

The first time you open Nadir fix, the phone gets the fill from Google Play. It's about 100 MB, so Wi-Fi is best. You'll see how far it has got at the top.

1. Tap **Auto fix** at the top right. It does every 360° that's **Not fixed** or **Check again**, one at a time, and shows how long is left. Locked 360°s wait until you unlock them. **Cancel** stops after the one it's on.
2. Open any 360° where the tripod or shadow still shows. You'll see the ground straight down, with forward at the top.
3. Paint over what's left with **Brush**. Drawing a loop round a big shadow is enough: the inside is filled too. **Eraser** wipes off what you painted. **Size** changes the brush.
4. Tap **Remove**.

**Undo** and **Redo** at the top take back your last fix, an Auto fix too. **Preview** shows the 360° as it will go to Google. **Original** takes this 360°'s fix off completely. It asks first.

Each card says **Not fixed**, **Auto fixed** or **Fixed by hand**. **Check again** means the 360° was stitched again, so its fix was for the old one.

## Colour: give your tour a look

Pick a look at the top: **Original** (as it was), **Vivid**, **Honey** or **Vista**. Then tap **Apply … to all**. 360°s on **Custom** keep their own colour.

Open a 360° to change its colour on its own:

- Tap a look, or move **Brightness**, **Contrast**, **Saturation** or **Warmth**.
- Moving a slider makes the 360° **Custom**, starting from the look it had. Custom is your tour's own look: tap **Custom** on another 360°, or **Apply Custom to all**, to use it there.
- Double-tap a slider to set it back to 0.
- Press and hold the picture to see it without the colour.

A look never pushes a bright sky to pure white, or shadows to black, more than they already were.

## Going back a step

You can open Nadir fix or Colour again from the menu. If you change a 360° there, the steps after it go for that 360° only, and it needs them again. For example, change its nadir fix and its colour and blur go. You're asked first. Every other 360° keeps its work.

## When you're done

Tap **Mark Nadir fix done** or **Mark Colour done**. You don't have to do every 360°, and this is also how you skip a step. A 360° added later shows in the menu as new.

360°s added from Google Maps can't be changed. They show **From Google Maps**.

Already on Google Maps? A new fix or colour means publishing that 360° again, the same as a new blur. See [Publish to Google Maps](tour-publish.md).
