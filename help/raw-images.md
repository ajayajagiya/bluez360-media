# Raw source images

Raw data is what your 360 was built from: the single shots from Photo mode, or the recording from a video sweep.

To see it, long-press a 360 in its area and tap **View raw data**.

- **Photo mode:** shots are listed in the order you took them, grouped by row (Horizon, Upper Horizon, Zenith, Lower Horizon, Nadir). Switch between **List** and **Grid**, or swipe. Tap a shot to see it full screen and swipe through the whole set.
- **Video sweep:** the **Sweep recording** comes first, with its size, then the frames taken from it. **No frames on this phone yet** is normal before stitching; stitching pulls them out of the recording.

**Freeing up space.** You can't delete single shots, because a set with a hole in it can't be stitched. Long-press instead:

- a shot › **Delete all 66 shots** (the number matches your set). This can't be undone, and the 360 can't be stitched again.
- the recording › **Delete the recording**. The frames stay, and stitching again reuses them.
- a frame › **Delete all … frames**. The recording stays, so the frames can be pulled out again.

If you delete both the recording and the frames, the 360 can't be stitched again. Deleting raw data doesn't remove your finished 360 image.
