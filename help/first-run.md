# The first-run guide

The first time you reach the Gallery, the app asks **First time here?**

- Tap **Show me** for a short walk-through. You do each step yourself; there's nothing to read.
- Tap **Skip** to go straight to the app.

This question is asked only once.

## What the walk-through covers

A highlighted button and a hand show you what to tap next:

1. **Add a project**, and pick its location on the map (you can skip this part).
2. **Open the project**, then **Add an area** and **Open the area**.
3. **Shoot a 360** and take your first shot.
4. **Open the project explorer**, then **Open your account** and choose **Account Settings**.
5. **Pick a theme to finish**.

If you close the app part way, the walk-through carries on where you left off. It can't be started again once it's finished or skipped.

## Replaying the tips: How to use

Each main screen has its own short tips. To see them:

1. Go to the screen you want help with.
2. Tap your account icon at the top of the screen.
3. Tap **How to use**.

Tap **Next** to move through the tips, **Got it** on the last one, or **SKIP** to close them.
