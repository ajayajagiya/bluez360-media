# Blueezee

Blueezee is Bluez360's help assistant, your 360° guide. Her name comes from **Bluez**360. She is an AI chat inside the app that answers questions about capturing, stitching, viewing, sharing and backing up your 360s.

## Open Blueezee

1. Tap your account icon at the top of the screen, then **Account Settings**.
2. Tap **Help & support**.
3. Type your question, or tap one of the suggested questions.

## What Blueezee can do

- Answer from these help pages only. Under each answer, **From:** shows the page it came from; tap it to read the whole page.
- Explain how to do something, and why or when you'd want to.
- Remember the last few messages, so you can ask a follow-up like "and how do I undo that?".

## What Blueezee can't do

- See your account, tokens, projects or 360s. She can tell you where in the app to look, for example your token balance on the **Tokens** card in **Account Settings**.
- Answer things the help pages don't cover. She says so, and **Contact us** lets you email our team.
- Make changes in the app for you, or fix a problem on your phone.

## Blueezee can make mistakes

Blueezee is an AI, so an answer can sometimes be wrong or miss a detail. If something matters, tap the **From:** page under the answer and check it there. If an answer doesn't help, try asking in a different way, or email us.

## Good to know

- She needs an internet connection.
- There's a daily limit on questions. When it's used up, you can ask again tomorrow.
- She answers in English.
- To start over, tap **⋮** then **Clear chat**.

## Your chat and privacy

- Your questions are sent to Google Gemini to write the answer. Google may use them to improve its products, and people at Google may read them, so don't type personal details.
- Your chat is kept on your phone only, for 7 days after your last message. Signing out or **Clear chat** removes it sooner. We don't save chats on our servers.
