# Share a 360° image

**Share image** sends your finished 360° as a normal JPG picture to any app on your phone, such as a chat app, email or cloud storage.

1. Press and hold a 360°, an area or a project to open its menu.
2. Tap **Share**.
3. Tap **Share image**.
4. Pick the app to send it with.

Sharing an area or a project sends every finished 360° in it at once.

## What gets left out

- **Locked** 360°s are not sent. You'll see a note like "Shared 3 of 5 · 2 skipped — locked". Unlock them first to include them.
- 360°s that are not stitched yet have no picture to send. If there's nothing to send, you'll see "Nothing to share yet."

## Good to know

- The picture carries 360° photo information, so apps that support 360° photos can show it as one. Other apps show it as a wide, flat picture.
- It can also include when and where it was taken, and your phone model.
- **Upload to cloud** in the same menu is marked **Coming soon**.
- To keep a full copy for yourself, including the original shots, use **Local backup** instead.
